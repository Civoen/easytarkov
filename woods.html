Woods

Duration 40 minutes PMC players 10-14 Enemies Scavs, Shturman
Points of Interest
LocationNotes
Sawmill Shturman's territory. His weapon box sits on a tyre against the central woodpile, always holding rare or superrare loot — needs his stash key.
Sniper Rock The large rock formation near the map's centre. Long, exposed sightlines — a major long-range danger zone.
Military Camp / USEC Checkpoint Contested mid-tier loot area, high foot traffic.
BTR patrol A neutral BTR-82A tours fixed stops on a timer. Offers a taxi service, "covering fire" for a safer drop-off, and found-in-raid item extraction via its trunk (delivered by mail after 1 hour). Becomes hostile only if attacked or after covering fire is purchased.
Boss
Shturman commands the Sawmill, protected by 2 heavily armed and armoured guards. His signature loot is the Red Rebel ice pick.
