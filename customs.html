Boss — Reshala
Reshala is Customs' resident Scav Boss, a former small-time fixer who consolidated power in the industrial zone once the city locked down. Unlike Tarkov's more militant bosses, he has no combat background and avoids direct fights, hanging back while 3-4 heavily armed guards (nicknamed the "Zavodskoy") do the fighting for him. He's identifiable by his brown sweater and civilian clothing, unlike his guards' blue jackets — and he never wears armor himself. His signature loot is a Golden TT pistol. He spawns most often around the dorms or the new gas station, occasionally at Fortress with a smaller escort.
